---
title: Shell, or on its own
description: The apps run alone. The shell is one Wayland desktop they can share.
---

Every Rivet app starts by itself. You do not need the shell, and you do not need the other apps.

The shell is for Wayland compositors that speak the layer-shell protocol: niri, Hyprland, Sway, river, labwc, Wayfire, and COSMIC. It is not a GNOME shell.

When you do use the shell, the dock starts with Files, Terminal, Edit, and Notes pinned. Files can be the Open and Save picker. The wallpaper colors reach the apps while they are open, so you do not restart them.

Startup snippets for niri, Hyprland, and Sway are in the [Shell README](https://github.com/ulinaaron/rivet-apps/blob/main/apps/shell/README.md).
